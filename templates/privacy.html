Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided at registration.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter when logging expenses.
  • Usage data — pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the Service.

We do not collect payment information, government-issued IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly experience. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Diagnose errors and improve the reliability of the Service.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core functionality of the Service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Your expense data is associated with your account and is not accessible to other users. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Please note that no method of electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. We may use the following categories of third-party services to operate the platform:

  • Hosting providers — to run and store the application infrastructure.
  • Analytics tools — to understand aggregate usage patterns (no individual data is shared).

Any third-party service we use is bound by its own privacy policy and applicable data protection laws. We do not control their practices and encourage you to review their policies independently.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to access the data we hold about you, or want to request deletion of your account, please reach out to us. We aim to respond to all privacy-related requests within 14 days.

You can contact us via the support link in your account settings or by emailing us directly at the address provided during onboarding.
